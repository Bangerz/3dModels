# Use survey and photographic evidence in Inverloch Explore

Identify the address or landmark, the features the pass will change, and the model revision being reviewed. Keep the evidence record with the existing street or landmark proposal. Use a small named group before purchasing, downloading or processing a wider survey.

## Resolve the footprint and the source rights

1. Pull a dated [OpenStreetMap extract via Overpass](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API) with `building` and `building:part` ways and relations. Record the bounding box and source timestamp. Use the [official map endpoint](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/API_v0.6#Retrieving_map_data_by_bounding_box:_GET_/api/0.6/map) as a bounded cross-check when an Overpass instance is stale or incomplete.
2. Match outlines to [Vicmap Address and Property](https://www.land.vic.gov.au/maps-and-spatial/spatial-data/vicmap-catalogue/vicmap-property) using address, parcel boundaries and polygon overlap. Reconcile parents, building parts and detached structures before drawing or creating collision. Inspect conflicting dates or outlines around redevelopment sites.
3. Fill real gaps with the [Microsoft building footprint release](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) after checking the local imagery epoch when it is known. Record the release and imagery date separately. Treat the released height as an ML estimate of mean height above ground. Keep unsupported properties on their existing fallback.
4. Resolve rights for the particular dataset or photo and for the intended output. A catalogue footprint or free viewer shows coverage. An asset licence establishes permission to derive or publish game geometry, textures and review media. Keep the OSM attribution and applicable ODbL obligations with the extract. Check [Google's imagery terms](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/#street-view) before using it as an in-viewer reference.

For each feature, record its address or landmark ID, the source object or capture ID, capture date or unknown, release date, source link and licence. Record the observed property, units, uncertainty and method. Use distinct evidence labels for surveyed measurements, mapped outlines, photographed details and estimates. Keep this record in the existing street proposal or model notes. Keep licensed raw data and contributor originals outside public Git unless their terms expressly allow redistribution.

## Measure roof geometry and ground separately

- Check the [ELVIS area index](https://www.ga.gov.au/scientific-topics/national-location-information/elvis-elevation-information-system) and the provider coverage layer against the exact parcels. Distinguish a catalogue listing, a licensed delivery and a verified usable sample. Confirm which buildings existed on the acquisition date. For Vicmap, identify the survey and tile or flight IDs through the [Coordinated Imagery Program](https://www.land.vic.gov.au/maps-and-spatial/imagery/elevation-data).
- For a roof pass, look for classified LAZ or LAS files, or a roof-resolving DSM and a compatible bare-earth DTM. A DHM may already provide height above ground. Check the horizontal datum, vertical datum, grid registration, acquisition dates, resolution, class codes and no-data areas before transforming or subtracting grids. An open bare-earth DEM is useful for grounding.
- Sample nearby ground separately from the roof. Use returns or surface cells inside the roof outline and account for roof overhang, vegetation and obvious rooftop equipment. Fit planes only where there are enough clean samples. Record eave height, ridge height, pitch and their reference ground where supported. Report coverage or fit quality so a canopy hit or a poorly sampled roof stays provisional.
- For [Geoscape](https://api-docs.geoscape.com.au/guides/buildings/quickstart), request the building IDs and capture fields plus a small licensed height and roof sample. Check which attributes are populated for each building. A reported maximum slope can guide a shape choice. Ridge layout still needs supporting geometry or photos.
- In the renderer, `HeightField.metresAt` is terrain elevation in AHD and `HeightField.yAt` is the rendered surface in world units, including vertical exaggeration and applicable terrain patches. Convert the survey measurement to a local physical height first. Anchor the model above its rendered ground at the intended position. Check the approach, wall corners, foundations and collider before considering a terrain change.

Use a small source sample to decide whether to extend the method. Specify the named parcels, their clipped extent, coverage and acquisition dates, roof-bearing products, datum and permitted derived outputs. Where a licensed survey cannot be obtained within the task, record what is accessible and which properties remain estimated. Continue with eligible evidence on other features.

## Capture and model a landmark

- Check [Mapillary imagery and attribution](https://help.mapillary.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001770409-CC-BY-SA-license-for-open-data) for the frontage, capture date and licence. For roof colour, identify the [Vicmap aerial imagery](https://www.land.vic.gov.au/maps-and-spatial/spatial-data/vicmap-catalogue/vicmap-imagery) layer or other licensed mosaic by capture and confirm permissions for the intended use. Check colour in context of lighting and shadows.
- Capture a straight-on façade, an oblique from each visible side, and closer joinery, entrance or roof views where they matter. For a freestanding landmark, use overlapping photos around the accessible sides and a known dimension for scale. Record the date, vantage points, photographer and publication permission.
- Use licensed archive and heritage images to check age, identity, shape and historical features. State when a photograph is historical or its capture date is unknown. Use approved drawings as design evidence and confirm the built condition before using them as measured geometry. Verify current tenants and signs separately from archive images.
- Compare the reference with the real Explore view before editing. Keep surveyed dimensions and visual proportions distinct. Adjust the smallest visible, supported feature. For landmark scans, use a mesh as modelling reference or simplify it for Explore. Keep authoring photos and scans outside the game payload, then credit any source used to derive an asset.

## Integrate, compare and hand off

Find the owner of the visible wall, roof, ground and collider in the current checkout. Norman Road uses the canonical Inverloch renderer on `claude/inverloch-combined` for native geometry. The detailed number 13 model has its own path. The town-centre clock and its plaza are authored in the Explore host `app.html`. Inspect the implementation before moving an object or rebuilding the embed. Follow the repository workflow for source and delivery PRs.

Capture the actual baseline and candidate at matched camera poses, lighting and preset. For a single landmark, capture the normal approach and the view that exposes the changed feature. Check relevant functions such as moving clock hands, night lighting or collision where the edit can affect them. For street work, include relevant sightlines and confirm that mapped shell replacement leaves a single correct collider. Record technical checks and human review separately.

Keep the named evidence gap and the smallest useful next input with the draft. Link the exact commit, reviewer comparison and source rights. Follow [town rollout](town-rollout.md) when scaling a proven method to another block.
