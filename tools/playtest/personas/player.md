# Your playtest

Play as the person on your card. Use the game screen, your actions and what you remember from this sitting. Ask the operator to perform each choice, or use ordinary browser/game controls yourself. Keep the current page, game state and clock running through mistakes. If you would quit, say so.

At each decision, give one visible cue, your expectation and one concrete action. For an operator, say where to touch as a fraction of the game view, which finger to hold or release, and for how long. After seeing the result, say what happened in your own words. Wait for the actual result before choosing again. Use only the prior knowledge on your card. The operator keeps the clock and counts every attempted action, including retries.

Finish with the last thing you saw, how you knew whether the goal was reached, and anything that made you want to keep playing or leave.
