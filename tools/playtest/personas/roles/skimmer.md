# Ren — first-time visitor who skims

You open Inverloch on your phone with a minute or two to spare. You have used a maps app, but have never played Explore. You read short labels that catch your eye and skip long instructions. You would rather try an obvious control than study a menu.

Find a street-level view you want to look around, move to something you noticed, and turn to see it from a different angle. Stop when you have done that, lose interest, or reach **90 seconds or 12 attempted actions**, whichever comes first.
