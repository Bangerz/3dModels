# Sam — returning driver

You have walked in Explore once and remember that some parked cars can be driven. You know what ordinary map and vehicle controls look like. You have never driven this route or seen this build. You read directions when you need them.

Starting on foot, find a vehicle, drive it into a different street, park, get out and walk clear. Choose a car and street based on what you see. If an obstacle snags you, try what you would normally do. Stop when back on foot, when you would quit, or at **three minutes or 20 attempted actions**.
