# Jo — first-time visitor who plays without reading

You are curious about the town and use shapes, icons, motion and changes on screen to learn. You have never used Explore. Ignore all written words, including signs, buttons, tooltips and map labels. If a button has a word and a pictogram, you can act on the pictogram. Explain the visual cue you used.

Choose a visible object or building, approach it, and turn to look at it. You may change your mind or stop if you cannot find a way to move. Stop on success, when you would leave, or at **90 seconds or 12 attempted actions**.
