# A’Beckett Street at Reilly Street, Inverloch

The opening shot looks east from the library intersection. The ground-level Google imagery at 7 and 11 Abeckett Street is labelled **October 2025**. The model uses the observed arrangement of buildings, shade trees, parked vehicles, paving and signs. Its geometry and textures are drawn for the viewer. Shop heights, widths between identifiable corners, street furniture and interior displays are visual estimates.

- From the Reilly junction, the zebra runs from a paved north approach under a tree to the planted kerb extension beside the bakery. The parked vehicles face the north kerb while those at the south shops run parallel to it. Dark event banners flank the crossing. [Street View looking east](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=rbCTBBTNtDyWgAYLj1aMkA&heading=96.31&pitch=4.17)
- The bakery has a charcoal pastry sign, timber-toned doors and a stone sill. Alex Scott’s red office directly adjoins its west side. The chemist opposite wraps both faces of the Reilly corner with a cream awning. [Bakery and Alex Scott](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=rbCTBBTNtDyWgAYLj1aMkA&heading=185&pitch=5) · [Pharmacy](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=rbCTBBTNtDyWgAYLj1aMkA&heading=48&pitch=2) · [Bakery address](https://www.paulthepieman.com/) · [Pharmacy address](https://terrywhitechemmart.com.au/stores/terrywhite-chemmart-inverloch)
- The south-side shops step west under a run of terracotta gables. The library block to the north opens onto a lawn, the memorial and the Rotary clock. The marked accessible space in front of the Hub connects to the kerb-side approach. [South-side panorama](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=A7rFfS7DKxWbhAdfe41eqg&heading=175&pitch=2) · [North-side panorama](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=A7rFfS7DKxWbhAdfe41eqg&heading=2&pitch=1) · [Council accessible parking inventory](https://www.basscoast.vic.gov.au/assets/general-images/Social-and-Community-Planning/2022-Designated-Accessible-Disabled-Car-Parks-Information-Sheet-Updated-September-2022.PDF)
- The library's blue fascia and glazed west wing end in a round window on the return. A canopy covers the recessed entrance. A handrail runs beside the approach and the yellow visitor-information sign stands apart from the wall. [Official exterior photograph](https://images.bookeasy.com.au/website/thumbs/800_600_crop/bass/56743415-primary-2c76ba56-82bf-4a14-8c75-24a6d5d7e3dd.jpg) · [Visit Bass Coast](https://www.visitbasscoast.com.au/inverloch-visitor-information-centre) · [Hub floor plan](https://bookableblob.blob.core.windows.net/folderdocuments/f094f9e8-c465-470a-83af-f4697d93ada9-Inverloch%20Hub%20Plan.pdf)
- Toward Williams Street, the southern frontage passes the butcher, real-estate office and drive-through to the Esplanade Hotel. The hotel’s higher cream frontage and silver roof close the view.
